Diamonds

Production notes

Released November 10, 2017, Diamonds is a career-spanning greatest hits compilation commemorating 50 years of the songwriting partnership between Elton John and lyricist Bernie Taupin. Their historic collaboration began in 1967 after both responded to a talent advertisement in New Musical Express. The working relationship was famously separate: Taupin wrote lyrics in isolation, and Elton John set them to music at the piano, often in a matter of minutes without modifying the lyrics. Diamonds serves as the modern successor to earlier classic compilations like Greatest Hits (1974) and To Be Continued… (1990).

The 2-LP vinyl edition contains 21 tracks, serving as a streamlined, chronological sequence of their most essential work. It begins with Elton’s 1970 breakthrough “Your Song” and covers his chart-dominating 1970s run, featuring “Tiny Dancer,” “Rocket Man,” “Crocodile Rock,” “Daniel,” “Saturday Night’s Alright (For Fighting),” “Goodbye Yellow Brick Road,” “Candle in the Wind,” and “Bennie and the Jets.” It continues through his 1980s adult-contemporary triumphs like “I’m Still Standing,” “I Guess That’s Why They Call It the Blues,” “Nikita,” and “Sacrifice,” and concludes with his 1994 Oscar-winning The Lion King ballad “Can You Feel the Love Tonight” alongside the 2003 Ashley Beedle remix of “Are You Ready For Love.”

The collection features audio remastered by Bob Ludwig at Gateway Mastering. The lacquers for the vinyl edition were cut at Abbey Road Studios by Sean Magee, a veteran mastering and cutting engineer known for high-profile projects including the Beatles mono and stereo vinyl remastering campaigns.

About this 2019 Europe pressing (2×LP, Reissue, Remastered, Gatefold, 180g): Virgin EMI Records/UMC/Rocket Entertainment catalog 00602557681949 (with set catalog number 5768194 on the spine and jacket). This 2019 reissue was pressed by Optimal Media GmbH in Germany, which is identified by the BJ70373 runout matrix prefix (with LP1 carrying 5768196 and LP2 carrying 5768197 in the deadwax). This Optimal pressing is distinct from the 2017 European first pressing (r11128136), which was pressed by GZ Media in the Czech Republic (deadwax containing 161090E).

Housed in a sturdy gatefold jacket, the album includes printed inner sleeves with full lyrics and credits. This 2019 reissue does not include a download card, which distinguishes it from the 2017 first pressing and is reflected by the omission of the download card reference in the front sticker text.
