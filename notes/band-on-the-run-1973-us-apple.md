Released November 1973 on Apple Records, *Band on the Run* is the third studio album by Paul McCartney and Wings and the record that turned the band's fortunes around. Down to a trio—Paul, Linda McCartney, and Denny Laine—after two members quit on the eve of the sessions, the group decamped to EMI's studio in Lagos, Nigeria to record much of the album under famously difficult conditions. Paul handled drums and much of the instrumentation himself, and the result became one of the most acclaimed and commercially successful records of his post-Beatles career, yielding the hits "Jet," "Helen Wheels," and the title track. Orchestral arrangements were contributed by Tony Visconti.

**About this 1973 US Apple pressing (LP, Stereo):** Apple Records catalog `SO-3415`, released November 26, 1973. Discogs release [r30091652]. An original US pressing, ℗1973 EMI Records, issued with the one-sided color poster and a printed inner sleeve. Note the US/Canada-specific quirk: "Helen Wheels" was added to the North American editions, and while it is listed on the outer sleeve back, the printed inner sleeve omits its lyrics. The spine credits only "Paul McCartney," dropping the Wings name that appears on the labels.

The lacquers were cut at Capitol—every runout carries the stamped **MASTERED BY CAPITOL** (the rest of each matrix is hand-etched). This particular copy is the variant whose **Side A alone** was cut by the engineer signing `Wly`, the initials attributed to Capitol mastering engineer Wally Traugott; Side B carries a different cut. Your Side A runout reads `SW1 3415 F10 Wly #9 MASTERED BY CAPITOL`—the `SW1` cut designation and the `#9` stamper number are what distinguish this pressing from its otherwise-identical siblings (a near-twin, [r471170], carries the same `SW1 3415 F10 Wly` on Side A but `#7`, and has the Wly cut on both sides).
