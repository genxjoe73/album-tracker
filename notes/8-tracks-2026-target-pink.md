Released on June 5, 2026 by Columbia Records / Sony Music, *8-Tracks* is a curated compilation album celebrating the peak 1970s output of Pink Floyd (from 1971's *Meddle* through 1979's *The Wall*). The release is both a play on words—featuring exactly eight iconic tracks—and a tribute to the classic 8-track cartridge tape format. To create a cohesive and seamless listening experience, Steven Wilson was brought in to edit and crossfade the track sequence, utilizing sound effects and transition elements sourced directly from the original multitrack master tapes. 

The primary highlight for collectors is the inclusion of the "8-Track Version" of "Pigs On The Wing." Originally recorded to bridge Part 1 and Part 2 of the track on the 1977 *Animals* 8-track cartridge, this version features a bridging acoustic guitar solo by Snowy White. This marks the first time this rare edit has been officially issued on vinyl, CD, or digital streaming. The sequence flows smoothly across other classic tracks including "One of These Days," "Wot's... Uh The Deal," "Money," and "Comfortably Numb."
